I'm a Senior Technical Support Specialist at Namecheap — one of the world's largest web hosting and domain registrars. Every day I troubleshoot Linux-based servers, resolve DNS failures, debug email protocols, and keep production environments running for thousands of customers.

That operational depth — knowing what breaks, why it breaks, and how to fix it fast — is exactly what makes a great DevOps engineer. Now I'm formalising that with a structured DevOps certification and building real projects that demonstrate the full pipeline: code, containerise, automate, deploy, monitor.

I'm not starting from scratch. I'm building upward.
